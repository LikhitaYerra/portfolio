About

I have completed the taught programme and PFE of my Master’s in AI & Data Science at aivancity in Paris, finishing with Mention Très Bien. By day I ship production AI as an engineer apprentice at IAware/amzilai. My research asks not only what models can do, but how deployment affects people and institutions: calibration under knowledge expiry, multi-objective LLM tradeoffs (quality, latency, energy, cost), and identity-preserving generation with hard metrics.

I am looking for a PhD in machine learning, on the question of when model outputs and iterates should be trusted and how to measure that trust carefully.

  • Calibration and uncertainty
  • Temporal knowledge decay
  • Multi-objective LLM evaluation
  • Trustworthy RAG
  • Representation learning
  • Digital twins and control
